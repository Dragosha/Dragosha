Look, your loot!

android defold game html5 ios

This is a card-based dungeon crawler game with magic and simple rules. You will receive a set of cards, one of which represents your hero. You can move your hero one step left, right, up, or down to navigate through dungeons, unlock chests, collect coins, drink elixirs, cast fireballs, avoid traps, and defeat bosses. The game has basic mechanics with roguelike elements, making it challenging to master. Defeating bosses adds difficulty to the game and upgrades your hero, while opening chests rewards you with loot. However, be careful as there are also dangerous cards, such as poison, that can hinder your progress. The game is said to be addictive and can provide hours of entertainment. 

  • App Store
  • Google Store
  • HTML 5: Play in your browser: Poki or Yandex games

There are 11 heroes to choose from, each with different tactics to help you collect as much loot as possible. These heroes include Knight, Mage, Paladin, Thief, Ruiner, Dark Druid, Sir Lancelot, Berserk, Assassin, Plague Doctor, and Archer.
