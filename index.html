My name is Igor Suntsev, I am a game developer. I started on ZX-Spectrum with small games. For a long time worked as a web designer and art director. Since 2009 I've been developing web games on Adobe Flash technology, in 2017 I moved to Defold engine.

I am a multi-instrumentalist: drawing, programming, design. Collaborated with a lot of game portals such as: Poki, Armor Games, Mahjonggames.com, Y8, Candystand, Miniclip, Kizi, Notdoppler, Yahoo games and many others.
